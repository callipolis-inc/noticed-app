1. Our Privacy Philosophy

At Noticed, we hold the conviction that journals, field reflections, manuscripts, and private thoughts are sovereign territory. We architected Noticed around an offline-first, client-sovereign model:

  • Your manuscripts and recordings reside on your device.
  • We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal data.
  • We do not employ advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or cross-app surveillance networks.
  • We do not use your private notes to train generative AI foundation models.

2. Information We Collect and Process

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

By default, all your creative output is stored directly within your device's browser sandboxed IndexedDB database and local storage:

  • Manuscripts & Notes: Note bodies, titles, companion marginalia (sidenotes & footnotes), inline highlights, and custom paragraph styles.
  • Notebooks & Spaces: Space names, theme color palettes, cover foil textures, spine patterns, and typography preferences.
  • Media Attachments: Photo files, analog darkroom development parameters, video clips, and voice memos.
  • Preferences: Active theme (Alabaster, Obsidian, Sage, etc.), typography scale, and haptic feedback toggles.

B. Account & Cloud Synchronization Data (Optional)

If you voluntarily choose to enable Cloud Synchronization to back up and sync notes between devices:

  • Authentication Data: Your email address and securely hashed authentication credentials managed through Supabase.
  • Synchronized Content: Encrypted copies of your spaces, notes, and media attachments uploaded to our secure cloud database and storage buckets.
  • Profile Details: Your chosen author name/pseudonym and avatar image.

You may use Noticed indefinitely without ever creating a cloud account.

C. Device Permissions

Noticed requests hardware permissions strictly when initiated by you for explicit creative features:

  • Microphone: Requested only when you record a Voice Memo or activate Voice Dictation. Audio recordings are stored locally. Live dictation utilizes your device's native speech framework. Audio is never scanned for marketing.
  • Camera & Photo Library: Requested only when you capture or attach a photograph or video clip to a note. We never access unselected items in your library.
  • Haptic Engine: Used to generate subtle mechanical feedback for tactile note interactions (can be deactivated anytime in Settings).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Render, format, and safeguard your notes, marginalia, and darkroom media.
  • Synchronize your manuscripts across authorized personal devices when Cloud Sync is active.
  • Allow you to generate complete, portable JSON backups and exported Markdown/PDF documents.
  • Verify your Atelier Pro license status if you subscribe or purchase lifetime access.

4. Data Storage, Security & Retention

Security Architecture

Local Storage: Your data is isolated in your device's browser sandbox (IndexedDB).

Cloud Security: When Cloud Sync is active, data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3, and data at rest is protected with AES-256 encryption via Supabase infrastructure.

Retention: Local content remains until you delete it or tap "Reset All Data". Cloud content remains until you delete the note or close your account.

5. Your Rights & Data Sovereignty (GDPR & CCPA Compliant)

Regardless of jurisdiction, every Noticed user has complete authority over their data:

  • Right to Portability & Export: Export your complete database at any time in open JSON format via Settings > Export Archive.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Instantly wipe all local data using the built-in "Reset All Data" safety feature in Settings, or request deletion of all cloud sync data.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit, overwrite, or delete any note, media attachment, or author profile directly within the app.
  • Right to Revoke Permissions: Revoke microphone, camera, or haptics at any time via device settings.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We restrict third-party service providers to essential infrastructure:

  • Supabase Inc.: Cloud database, authentication, and encrypted file storage (when sync is enabled).
  • Native Operating System Speech Framework: Web Speech API for on-device voice dictation.

7. Children's Privacy

Noticed is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Any such data will be deleted immediately upon discovery.
