Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 13, 2026 • Sovereign Offline-First Architecture

Noticed: Private Field Notes (“Noticed”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is built on a core principle: your personal thoughts, journals, and marginalia belong exclusively to you.

1. Local-First & Offline by Default

By default, all notebooks, field notes, photos, and voice memos created in Noticed are stored locally on your device using encrypted browser/native storage (IndexedDB & local storage). You can use Noticed indefinitely without ever creating an account or transmitting data to our servers.

2. Optional Cloud Synchronization

If you choose to sign up for a Noticed Cloud account, we collect only the minimum information required to synchronize your data across your devices:

  • Email Address: Used strictly for account authentication, password recovery, and security notifications.
  • Synchronized Notebooks & Notes: Stored securely in our cloud database (powered by Supabase with Row-Level Security) so only your authenticated session can read or modify your records.

3. Zero Tracking & Zero Third-Party Advertising

We do not use third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking pixels, or data brokers. We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal notes or metadata.

4. Device Permissions

  • Camera & Photo Library: Requested only when you explicitly attach a polaroid photo to a field note.
  • Microphone: Requested only when you record an audio voice memo inside a note.

5. Account & Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data at all times:

  • In-App Instant Deletion: Open Noticed → tap your Profile icon → scroll to Account & Data Privacy → tap Delete Account & Data. This permanently purges your cloud profile, all synchronized notebooks, notes, and local caches immediately.
  • Web Deletion Portal: You may also review instructions or submit a deletion request via our Account Deletion Portal.

6. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries or support requests, contact us at privacy@noticed.app.
