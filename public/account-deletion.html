Account & Data Deletion

Compliance: Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v) • Google Play User Data Safety

Users of Noticed: Private Field Notes (com.syntheticae.noticed) can permanently delete their cloud account and all associated personal data at any time.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the Noticed application.
  2. Tap the Author Profile button in the top navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account & Data Privacy section.
  4. Tap Delete Account & Data and confirm by typing DELETE.

This triggers our automated database procedure (delete_user_account), which immediately and irreversibly purges your identity, email record, all synchronized spaces, field notes, and local device caches.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you no longer have access to the app, you may request permanent account deletion via email from the address associated with your account:

Send Deletion Request Email →

What Data Is Deleted?

  • Authentication Identity: Your email address, password hash, and active session tokens in auth.users.
  • Cloud Content: All notebooks (public.spaces), field notes, media attachments (public.field_notes), and author profiles (public.profiles).
  • Retention Period: 0 days for in-app deletion (immediate cryptographic erasure); within 48 hours for manual email requests.
