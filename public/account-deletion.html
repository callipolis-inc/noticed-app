Account & Data Deletion

Compliance: Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v) & Google Play User Data Policy

Users of Noticed: Private Field Notes (`app.noticed.mobile`) can permanently delete their cloud account and all associated personal data at any time.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the Noticed mobile or web application.
  2. Tap the Author Profile button in the top navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account & Data Privacy section.
  4. Tap Delete Account & Data and confirm by typing DELETE.

This executes our automated database purge (delete_user_account), which immediately and irreversibly deletes your user account, email record, all synchronized notebooks, field notes, and local device caches.

Method 2: Web Request (If You Uninstalled the App)

If you no longer have access to the app, you may request permanent account deletion via email from the address associated with your account:

Send Deletion Request Email →

What Data Is Deleted?

  • Authentication Identity: Your email address, password hash, and active session tokens in auth.users.
  • Cloud Content: All spaces/notebooks (public.spaces), field notes and attachments (public.field_notes), and author profile metadata (public.profiles).
  • Retention Period: 0 days for in-app deletion (immediate cryptographic erasure); within 48 hours for manual email requests.
